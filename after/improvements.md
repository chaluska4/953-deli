# 953 Deli — what this concept changes

Local redesign only. The `before/` screenshots are unchanged. Ordering still happens on the existing site.

## What the page does now

- The header stays one row: the real 953 Deli logo, Menu, Visit Us, and a gold Order Online button. On a phone those four items fit without a hamburger or a second Order Online stacked under the hero copy.
- The first screen is the name, one sentence, View Menu, Order Online, and the shop’s own cheesesteak photo. Both buttons sit on screen at 390×844 and in a short landscape view.
- Featured sandwiches and the full menu use names and prices taken from the current online menu. Items without a trustworthy photo are text only. The same food photo is not repeated across cards.
- Menu categories wrap onto new lines, so labels such as “Breakfast sandwiches” stay whole. Each price sits on the same line as its name.
- Visit Us puts the address, the phone number `(484) 221-8953`, Get Directions, and hours in one block.
- Every Order Online button uses the same link: the live menu with `dialogState=orderDetails`. Pickup, payment, and rewards stay on that site. Rewards links to the existing login page. There is no checkout on this concept.
