## Five changes that would help most

1. **Make hours and “open now” agree.** The location card lists every day, including Wednesday, as Closed, and the footer of that card says “Today: Closed.” The menu header and the Order details dialog say “Open now” and offer Pickup ASAP (25 min). Delivery is labeled unavailable in the same dialog. A guest cannot tell whether the shop is taking orders.

2. **Let the menu be scanned.** Specialty sandwiches sit in uneven cards: Ham and Cheese, Turkey, and Honey Turkey are text only, while Italian and Chicken Bacon Ranch share a similar burger photo that does not match the written item. Descriptions run together (“tomatoe , mayo”, “sal,t pepper”, “with with”). On a phone, the category row clips to “Breakfast Sandw…”, “Delivery unavailable” clips to “Delivery un…”, and a sticky “Start order” bar covers the next item’s price. The add control is a small plus.

3. **Shorten the path from “Order online” to the food.** The homepage spends its first screens on a full-bleed hero and photo bands. The first menu link is a “View menu” button after those bands. Choosing Order online then opens an Order details sheet that repeats Pickup, the address, and ASAP versus Schedule — choices already shown on the menu page. On a phone that sheet covers the sandwiches.

4. **Give the phone screen one clear action and a readable title.** The hero eyebrow “Best Deli in Allentown” is 14px on a 390px screen (28px on desktop). The line people can actually read is the serif subhead, “New York-style cheesesteaks on West Walnut Street.” The mobile header is a “Menu” pill; Order online sits at the bottom of the opened menu. Just under the hero, two gold Order online buttons stack around a single sentence.

5. **Put the address, phone, and hours in one trustworthy block.** The address renders as “953 Walnut St #963 #963” in the menu header and in the order dialog. The location card leads with a map and the all-closed schedule. The phone number, (484) 221-8953, sits outside that card. Above it, the Rewards panel fills only the left half of the desktop row.
