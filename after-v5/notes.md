# Catering, gift cards, and hiring pages

Local routes:

- http://localhost:5173/catering
- http://localhost:5173/gift-cards
- http://localhost:5173/careers

Header, mobile menu, and footer open these pages. Request Catering, Buy a Gift Card, Check Balance, and Apply Now still go to the existing Owner.com forms. The production build completed.

## Not published, so not on these pages

- Catering packages, prices, minimums, or a delivery area. The request page does say 24 hours’ notice, that dietary needs should be discussed by phone, and that plates, napkins, and cutlery are not included.
- Gift-card expiration or in-store redemption rules. The purchase page says the cards are for online pickup or delivery orders, and lists $10, $25, $50, $75, $100, plus a custom amount from $5 to $2,000.
- Job titles, wages, or benefits. The application is a general form: name, email, phone, a note, and a PDF resume. The suite on that form is still unconfirmed.
